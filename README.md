# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.
# THEORY
Self-driving cars are vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to drive with limited or no continuous human control. These cars use technologies such as Machine Learning, Computer Vision, sensors, cameras, radar, and GPS to understand their surroundings. Computer Vision helps identify roads, traffic signals, pedestrians, and other vehicles. Machine Learning helps the car recognize patterns and improve its decision-making. Sensors and cameras collect information about nearby objects, while GPS helps determine the vehicle's location and route. AI processes this information to control steering, acceleration, and braking. Examples of companies developing self-driving car technology include Tesla and Waymo. These technologies aim to improve road safety, reduce human driving errors, and provide convenient transportation.
# WORKING PRINCIPLE
Self-driving cars use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to observe their surroundings and make driving decisions. First, cameras and sensors collect information about roads, traffic signals, pedestrians, and other vehicles. Computer Vision processes the collected images to identify objects and road conditions. Machine Learning helps the car analyze the information and decide when to accelerate, brake, or turn. GPS helps determine the car's location and select the correct route. The AI system then controls the steering, speed, and braking to move the vehicle safely. For example, when a pedestrian crosses the road, the car detects the pedestrian and applies the brakes if necessary. Thus, AI helps self-driving cars navigate roads with reduced human intervention.
# RESULT
Thus, the application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was studied successfully. The study helped us understand how AI technologies such as Machine Learning, Computer Vision, sensors, and GPS enable vehicles to detect their surroundings, make driving decisions, and navigate roads with reduced human intervention.
